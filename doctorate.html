Programme Structure

DBA Programme Curriculum

A comprehensive and rigorous curriculum designed for global business leaders.

Entrepreneurial foundation

9 ECTS

Business Strategy & Entrepreneurship

Combines principles of strategic management with the entrepreneurial mindset required to launch and grow new ventures. Learn to identify opportunities, secure resources, and design innovative business models.

International Business Management

Focuses on managing businesses across borders in an interconnected global economy. Explore global trade theories, cross-cultural management, and strategies for global competitiveness.

Organization Design & Development

Examine how organizational structures, culture, and processes influence performance. Covers change management, leadership development, and performance improvement.

Strategic Business Management

9 ECTS

Business Development

Strengthen ability to drive growth in local and global markets. Master innovative approaches to scaling businesses and creating sustainable growth opportunities.

Strategic Management & Business Policy

Craft and evaluate strategies for long-term success. Integrates concepts of strategic analysis, competitive advantage, and corporate policy-making.

Business Analysis & Statistics

Introduces advanced statistical tools for business decision-making. Apply techniques to analyze complex problems and turn raw data into insights.

Governance & International Environment

9 ECTS

Good Governance for Corporate

Explore principles of corporate governance. Analyze board structures, leadership ethics, and accountability mechanisms that build stakeholder trust.

Legal Compliance

Understanding international and corporate legal compliance systems. Identify risks, implement programs, and ensure ethical operations.

International Business Environment

Examine external factors influencing global trade. Study economic systems, cultural dynamics, and political risks to navigate cross-border challenges.

Financial Management

9 ECTS

Corporate Finance & Accounting

In-depth understanding of financial management principles and corporate accounting practices. Manage capital, financial risks, and investment opportunities.

Advanced Accounting & Financial Strategy

Focus on advanced accounting methods and financial strategies. Topics include performance measurement, cost management, and strategic budgeting.

Governance, Audit & Financial Compliance

Role of governance and audit in maintaining integrity. Develop skills needed to uphold accountability and transparency in business operations.

Research Methodology

9 ECTS

Focuses on advanced techniques for analyzing quantitative empirical data, with special emphasis on second-generation statistical approaches and practical applications.

Philosophy

9 ECTS

Process of Applied Business Research: Introduces fundamental principles and development process of managerial business research.

Experiments

9 ECTS

Survey Design: Overview of methods and practices for creating and utilizing data based on experiments and surveys in research.

Scholarly 30 ECTS

Conference/Seminar: Attendance in conferences and certifications to enhance knowledge and network with peers.

Doctoral 60 ECTS

Thesis: In-depth research and production of a doctoral thesis that meets program requirements.

Viva 30 ECTS

Defense: Present and defend doctoral thesis in a viva voce examination with external examiners.

Mentorship

Role of the Supervisor

The designated supervisor’s role in the program is similar to that of a standard Doctorate Project supervisor. However, their focus is primarily on mentoring the candidate, providing support and guidance during the preparation of research material for examination (oral defense), and overseeing the examination process.

The supervisor’s responsibilities include:

  • Assisting the candidate in the selection of suitable publications for inclusion in the submission.
  • Providing guidance and advice to the candidate in relation to thesis preparation.
  • Advising the candidate on any necessary research training requirements.
  • Supporting the candidate through the examination (oral defense) process.

Submission Requirement

Crucial steps to finalize your academic contribution.

Submit a stand-alone or compilation of research-based written work, which must be between a minimum of 20,000 words and a maximum of 25,000 words

Defend the submission in front of a panel of experts, which may be conducted online via video conferencing.

Demonstrate that the scholar has conducted a comprehensive and critical investigation of a specific area of research and made an independent and original contribution to the existing body of knowledge as well as professional practice.
